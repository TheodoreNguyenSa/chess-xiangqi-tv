# Chess & Xiangqi TV — Cờ Vua & Cờ Tướng TV

Play **Chess** and **Xiangqi (Chinese chess)** on your TV: against the computer at 5 levels, with a friend on the same remote, or — the fun part — **two players on their phones while the big board stays on the TV**.

Made by **Toan Nguyen In Oz** — free apps and content for the Vietnamese community in Australia.

## Features

- **Two games in one app:** international Chess and Xiangqi, both with full rules (castling, en passant, promotion, flying generals, stalemate-is-a-loss in Xiangqi…)
- **Play against the TV:** built-in engine with **5 levels** — Level 1 is friendly and blunders, Level 5 searches deepest. Pick your side (White/Red, Black, or random), 15-minute clocks, hint button, take-back, resign
- **Play on your phones:** the host sets a 4-digit room code on the TV, both players scan the QR code with their phones (same home Wi-Fi, no internet, no account) and play on the phone screen while everyone watches the big board on TV. Extra phones can join as spectators
- **Two players, one remote:** pass-and-play on the TV itself
- **Famous games library:** 29 annotated games and studies (12 chess + 17 xiangqi) — the Opera Game (1858), the Immortal Game (1851), the Evergreen Game (1852), the Game of the Century (1956), Kasparov's masterpiece (1999), Steinitz's Immortal (1895), Rubinstein's Immortal (1907), Fischer's 21-move brilliancy (1963), the Légal Trap, the Saavedra study, composed Anastasia and Arabian mate studies, classic Xiangqi openings (Central Cannon vs Screen Horses, Same-Direction, Opposite and Advisor-Corner Cannons) and nine mate-in-1 Xiangqi endgame studies
- **Step-by-step commentary in Vietnamese:** replay every game move by move (or auto-play), with an explanation of the key moves
- **"What if…?" branches & quiz mode:** at the turning points the app asks *you* to pick the move — choose wrong and it plays out the alternative line and explains why it fails
- **Bilingual:** Tiếng Việt / English, remembered between sessions
- Background music with a MUTE-key toggle, auto-update from GitHub releases

Everything runs on the TV — the chess engines are built into the app, no internet needed except for updates.

## Install on your TV

The app is a standard Android TV APK. Pick the easiest method:

### Option A — Downloader (AFTVnews)
1. Install **Downloader** from the Play Store (Android TV / Google TV) or Amazon Appstore (Fire TV).
2. Open Downloader and enter the code from the latest release (or the APK link).
3. Tap **Go** to download, then **Install** (allow "Install unknown apps" for Downloader if asked).

### Option B — Send Files to TV
1. Install **Send Files to TV** on your TV and on your phone.
2. Send the APK file from your phone to the TV, then open it with a file manager and tap **Install**.

### Option C — USB
1. Copy the APK onto a USB drive (FAT32).
2. Plug it into the TV, open a file manager, tap the APK and install.

Requires Android 5.0+ (Android TV / Google TV). The app appears as **Chess & Xiangqi TV** in your apps.

## Install on your phone

The same APK also installs on an Android phone (v1.1.0+): download the APK from the
[Releases](../../releases) page, open it and allow "Install unknown apps" for your
browser if asked. The app appears in your app drawer as **Chess & Xiangqi TV** and
runs in landscape with full touch controls — tap a piece, then tap where it goes.
On the phone you can play against the computer (5 levels), pass-and-play with a
friend, and browse the famous-games library. (Hosting a phone-play room still works
best on the TV, where everyone can see the big board.)
